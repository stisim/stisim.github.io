lead: >-
  Evaluation of two new syphilis diagnostics: one detecting syphilis from genital
  ulcer disease, and a confirmatory test used after an initial positive treponemal result.
pathogens: [SYPH, HIV]
themes: [Diagnostic evaluation, Point-of-care testing, Cost-effectiveness]
setting: Cross-cutting / methods
findings:
  - >-
    Treponemal tests stay positive after a prior infection resolves, so scaling up
    dual HIV/syphilis testing was set to drive sharp increases in overtreatment.
  - The new diagnostics produce meaningful reductions in overtreatment.
  - >-
    They are cost-effective at certain price points, provided the diagnostic cost
    stays below a threshold share of the overtreatment costs it avoids.
