  discharge syndrome, detecting NG, CT and TV from symptomatic presentation,

findings:
  - Substantially reduces overtreatment relative to syndromic management.
  - >-
    Impact on undertreatment is highly uncertain, hinging on how syndromic
    management operates on the ground, which remains poorly characterised.
  - Net health benefit can't be settled without better field evidence on current practice.
