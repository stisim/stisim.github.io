Downstream value of point-of-care tests

Building on the multiplex and syphilis work: what would it take for these point-of-care diagnostics to deliver a measurable improvement in health outcomes?

What we're tracking

  • Tracks downstream benefits such as higher partner-notification and care-seeking rates.
  • Considers whether diagnosis is associated with reduced risk behaviour afterwards.
  • Explores bundled prevention packages offered at the point of diagnosis.
